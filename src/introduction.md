# Introduction

<cite>OpenLoco</cite> is a free and open-source transport simulation. 
The towns and industries of the world both produce and require cargo. 
Your job is to provide transportation services to them using trains, 
trams, busses, trucks, aircraft and ships.

But not all is so simple. You will have to navigate harsh topography, 
adapt to old industries closing as new ones arise and cope with the 
growing demand for your services to succeed. You will also need to avoid 
own goals, including angering the authorities, congesting your networks 
and vehicles crashing into each other.

You start with a loan, a map, and town and industry directories and your own 
cunning. Can you come out on top and meet the challenges of the various 
scenarios?
