# Towns

Towns are a collection of buildings and roads. They are sources and 
destinations for cargo and provide infrastructure for companies to use. 
Providing towns with transport will earn their favour and help them grow 
into productive cities. However, destroying them or the local 
environment will earn their ire and they may forbid building demolition.

Town buildings produce passengers and mail. Larger buildings produce far 
more passengers than smaller ones. [Stations](stations.md) in towns can accept 
passengers and ones near groups of large buildings accept mail, food and 
goods.

Town roads can be used by transport companies. The smallest towns use 
dirt roads and brick and wooden bridges. Larger towns use sealed roads 
and superior bridges.

> [!TIP]
>
> A bus service in a town can be a cheap way to make money because the 
> roads are already built.

Each town will have a name displayed above its centre when the 'Town names 
displayed' option is enabled in the view menu. Clicking a town's name 
will show a status window. The left tab a view of the town and its 
population, the middle tab shows a chart of the town's population over 
time and the right tab shows the town authority's rating of each company 
that has worked in the area around it.

The first option of the towns and industries menu will display a table 
of towns alongside their type, population and the number of stations in 
them.

## Town Growth

Towns will slowly grow over time. This can be sped up by delivering 
cargo to them. As towns grow, they will upgrade roads, build more roads 
and buildings and upgrade existing buildings into larger ones. Towns 
will use of connecting roads by building along them and starting other 
roads on them.

> [!TIP]
>
> Transport services in small towns make little money. However, they can 
> make them grow into far cities that provide many passengers and much 
> mail and complete the food and goods supply chains. Thus, establishing 
> transport services in towns can be highly worthwhile. As this takes 
> time, this is less useful in scenarios with a short time limit.

## Local Authorities

Each town has its own local authority. Each authority evaluates the 
actions of the companies in the area around town. Local authorities like 
companies that plant trees around the town, dislike companies that knock 
down trees and houses and despise ones that demoish farmland. Their 
opinion of companies that provide transport services to the town will 
slowly improve. If a local authority dislikes your company too much, 
they will forbid you from demolishing buildings in that town.

> [!TIP]
>
> It is best to build through towns, before you build other 
> infrastructure around them if you need to.
