## Food

The primary food industries are the vineyard, wheat and livestock farms. 
Vineyards produce grapes, wheat farms produce grain and livestock farms 
produce livestock. All three farms' production depends on the number of 
surrounding fields. Fields that have trees or snow on them do not count 
towards production.

Wineries only accept grapes. Breweries and flour mills only accept 
grain. Food processing plants accept wheat and livestock. All four 
industries produce food, which may be transported to cities.

## Wood Products

Managed forests produce timber based on the number of surrounding 
fields. Snowy fields still produce timber.

Timber may be transported to a sawmill or paper mill. Sawmills produce 
goods while paper mills produce paper. Paper can be transported to 
printing works, which produce goods. Either way, the goods may be 
transported to cities.

## Factories

Factories produce goods based on the amount of steel and chemicals delivered
to them. Goods can be transported to cities.

Steel is produced by steel mills. These require both coal and iron ore. 
These are respectively produced by coal and iron ore mines, which 
require nothing. Coal may also be transported to power stations, but 
these produce nothing.

Chemicals come from chemical works, which require nothing.

## Oil

Oil wells and oil rigs both produce oil. Wells generate on land, while 
rigs generate over water. Either way, oil can be transported to 
refineries, which use it to produce goods. As always, goods can be 
transported to cities.

## Tourism

Ski centres produce passengers if passengers are transported to them. As 
they produce nothing unless transported passengers, one will need to 
transport passengers from a town to them.
